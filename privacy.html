Privacy Policy

Application: Lecture Player (Dr. Gendy) · Last updated: November 17, 2026

Lecture Player (Dr. Gendy) is an educational lecture delivery platform for enrolled medical students. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can request deletion of your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Identity Data: Your full name, verified email address, and Google Account identifier (when you choose Continue with Google), plus a platform-generated Student ID (e.g., DG-1001).
  • Course Activity: Your course subscription status, video resume timestamps (stored locally in your browser), and quiz submissions/scores.
  • Device & Content-Protection Data: A cryptographic hash derived from your IP address and browser User-Agent, active playback session identifiers, and security events (such as concurrent session detection or capture-protection triggers) to enforce our 2-Device Limit, single-session playback, and visible/forensic watermarks.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your account (via email OTP, password, or Google Sign-In) and manage access to lectures, PDFs, and quizzes.
  • To display your name and Student ID as an anti-piracy watermark during video playback and enforce the 2-Device Limit.
  • To send strictly transactional account emails (signup verification and password reset codes).

3. Data Sharing & Advertising

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling. We use only essential infrastructure providers to operate the service: Cloudflare (application hosting and storage), Backblaze B2 (encrypted media storage), Brevo (transactional OTP emails), and Google Identity Services (only if you choose Google Sign-In).
